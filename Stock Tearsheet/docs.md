
# Stock Analysis Sheet

This is my attempt at making a portfolio analysis web app. I may not be training to enter the quant space directly, but this is something that came to mind one day while learning more about stocks and finances. I am no finance major, but I have a basic understanding of how stocks operate, however in the future I would like to be someone who is financially literate to the point where it becomes second nature to understand the benefits of my financial decisions. In the financial world there is never one variable to signal that a stock/portfolio will perform well, and looking from the outside in can be really overwhelming, so I decided why not put together many of the metrics that professionals look at into an easy to understand and digestible website.
